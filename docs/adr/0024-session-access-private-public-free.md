# 0024: Who may join a session: private, public, free

- **Status:** accepted (2026-10-09). Extends 0015 and the floor rules in `crates/cha-streamer/src/viewers.rs`.
- **Context:** only an environment's owner and admins could connect to it (share links aside), and an owner or admin joining took the keyboard and mouse from whoever had them. Joining a session someone is using should not seize it, and an owner should be able to let other portal users in.
- **Decision:**
  - **Joining never seizes.** A session joining an environment takes the floor only when nobody holds it, the holder's page is idle (hidden and silent), or the holder is the same user (their other device). Otherwise it joins as a viewer and its page offers "Take control" (`floor.can_take`). This applies to owners and admins too.
  - **Access is per environment**, `environments.access`: `private` (the owner and admins, as before), `public` (any signed-in user but a guest may watch and ask) or `free` (they may watch and take the controls). The owner changes it while the environment runs (`PUT /api/environments/{id}/access`, audited as `environment.access_set`). A launch copies the owner's `sessionAccess` preference (`private` without one), set on the Sharing settings page. Everything existing stays private.
  - **Reach.** `GET /api/environments/joinable` lists other users' running public and free environments; `GET /api/environments/{id}` and `connect` accept them. Stop, share links, logs and access stay with the owner and admins.
  - **Streamer roles.** The media token's `role` gains `guest` (public) and `member` (free), and its claims an optional `name` (display name) for the holder's page. A guest never takes the floor on joining and takes it only from nobody; the holder hands it over (`give_control`) after a `request_control`. A member takes it from anyone. Either can `request_pad`; an owner or admin holding the floor answers with `give_pad` (slot 1 to 3) and takes it back with `revoke_pad`. The session is then a player (as a share link's) until revoked. The holder's `viewers` list carries each session's `request`, `granted` and `name`.
- **Consequences:** `private` is the default so nothing becomes visible by surprise. Public and free are for a household or friends on one portal; they are not a boundary between untrusted users, as a member can take the controls from the owner. The native player still treats a viewer as it did and offers "Take back".
