# Cha Portal Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

Thank you for contributing to Cha Portal. This agreement says what rights you give the project when you contribute. You keep the copyright in your work. You give the Maintainer a licence to use it, including the right to distribute it under licences other than the AGPL, and the Maintainer promises in return that it always stays available as free software.

**Why we ask.** Cha Portal is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Some places people install software from, such as Apple's App Store, add terms that the AGPL doesn't allow, so AGPL code can't ship there unless every copyright holder agrees. This agreement lets Cha Player ship there with your contribution in it.

- **"Maintainer"** means Alex Red, or a person or organisation that Alex Red designates in writing to carry on Cha Portal and that takes on the commitments in section 4.
- **"You"** means the individual, or the organisation on whose behalf an individual accepts this agreement, who submits a Contribution.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code, documentation, images and tests, that You submit to the Maintainer for inclusion in Cha Portal, through a pull request, patch, issue or any other means. A work You mark as "Not a Contribution" when You submit it is not one.
- **"Project"** means Cha Portal, at <https://github.com/ban-red/cha-portal>, and any work the Maintainer makes from it.

In return, the Maintainer commits that:

1. **Free software, always.** Every version of the Project that includes Your Contribution and that the Maintainer distributes, under any licence, is also published under the AGPL-3.0-or-later, or a later version of the GNU AGPL, with its complete source code, at no charge.

You provide Contributions "as is", without warranties of any kind, except as written in this section. You don't have to provide support for them.

## Accepting

The first time You open a pull request, a bot asks You to post this comment:

> I have read the Cha Portal CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

It records Your GitHub name and the date in the repository. You sign once; it covers later pull requests too. To contribute on behalf of an organisation, open an issue first so we can agree how the organisation signs.
